## ⚔️ Core Subsystem Highlights

1. **Sub-Tick Weapon Timing**:
   Replaces vanilla attack speed delay with direct integer tick overrides ($T_{\text{delay}}$). Swords default to 4 ticks ($5.0\text{ attacks/sec}$), Axes default to 8 ticks ($2.5\text{ attacks/sec}$), and Hoes default to 1 tick ($20.0\text{ attacks/sec}$). Setting $T = 0$ unlocks pure instantaneous 1.8-style click-spamming.

2. **Hotbar Swap Agility**:
   Enabling `ig:prevent_item_swap_cooldown` eliminates the artificial attack meter reset when cycling hotbar items, empowering rapid weapon combos.

3. **Multi-Sensory Combat Juice**:
   Attacks with $>80\%$ charge trigger dual particle bursts (`ParticleTypes.CRIT` and `ParticleTypes.ENCHANTED_HIT`) and pitch-shifted audio (`SoundEvents.PLAYER_ATTACK_STRONG`) scaling from $1.0\times$ up to $1.4\times$.
