# 📖 Combat Cooldown Adjuster: Player Guide

Welcome to the field manual for **Combat Cooldown Adjuster**. This mod is designed to give you absolute control over your combat rhythm.

## 🏁 Getting Started
1. **Install Dependencies**: Ensure you have **Fabric API** and **DasikLibrary** installed.
2. **Launch Game**: The mod works automatically with vanilla-like defaults.
3. **Configure**: Open your world's **Game Rules** menu to start tweaking.

- **Comboing**: Enable `Swap Agility` to switch between a sword strike and an axe strike instantly.
- **Juice**: Keep `Combat Juice` enabled for visual confirmation of high-charge hits.
